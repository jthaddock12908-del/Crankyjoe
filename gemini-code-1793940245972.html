Fair questions

Is this available yet?

Not yet — this page is for founding early access. Spanish launches first. Sign up and you'll get your first cram plan free when we open the doors, before any public launch.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Duolingo for free?

You can ask an AI to roleplay — but you have to design the plan, the drills, the recovery phrases, and the escalating difficulty yourself, every day, while anxious. CramLingo builds the whole countdown for you. Duolingo teaches a language; it has no idea you have a dinner in 21 days.

Can I really get ready in three weeks?

Ready for one conversation — yes. We won't promise fluency, and we won't pretend to. The goal is sounding competent and recovering gracefully in the specific situation you're facing. That's a trainable skill on a deadline.

What if my level is too low?

The plan adapts to your honest starting point. If you're a near-beginner, it leans harder on recovery phrases, listening, and short scripted openers — the things that keep a conversation alive at any level.

What languages do you cover?

Spanish first, because that's where the loudest need is. French and Portuguese follow. Tell us your language at signup and you'll be first in line when it ships.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Founding users pay nothing today — signing up reserves your spot and your free plan. When paid packs launch, they'll carry a simple refund policy: if you do the plan and don't feel readier, you get your money back.
